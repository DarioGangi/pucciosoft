Privacy Policy – Faroda

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Faroda is a single-player game published by Dario Gangi, an individual developer who publishes under the name PuccioSoft ("I", "me"), and who is the data controller for the data described below. This page explains what data the game uses, how it is captured, what I do with it, who it is shared with and how long I keep it.

1. What data is captured and how

a) Sign-in for achievements. To unlock achievements, the game asks you to sign in with your account on the store where you play it and requests only basic profile permission. The sign-in is handled by the store's own login screen, and the game never sees your password. I do not store your account ID, display name or any other sign-in data.

b) Anonymous gameplay statistics. When you complete a level or die, the game sends me a small record containing: date and time, level, collectibles found, completion percentage, time spent on the level, and (for deaths) the level where it happened. These records contain no name, account ID, email, IP address or any other identifier, and I cannot link them to you.

c) Local files. Save data and settings are stored on your computer only and are not sent to me.

2. How I use the data

The anonymous statistics are used only to understand how players progress and to balance and improve the difficulty and design of the game. I do not use them for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

I do not sell or share data with third parties for their own purposes. The anonymous statistics are stored in a spreadsheet hosted on Google Sheets, so Google LLC acts as my service provider for storing them.

4. Retention

Your account ID is not stored by me. Anonymous statistics are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Local files stay on your computer until you delete them.

5. Legal basis

Because the statistics are anonymous, they are not personal data. To the extent any data were considered personal, my legal basis is my legitimate interest in improving the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the GDPR, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). You can exercise them by writing to the address below.

7. Children

Faroda is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Changes

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact
