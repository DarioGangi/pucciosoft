Privacy Policy – Faroda

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Faroda is a single-player game developed by PuccioSoft ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses when you play it.

Information we collect

We do not operate any server for the game, and we do not collect, store or sell personal data about players.

Epic Games account

When you play Faroda through the Epic Games Store, the game asks you to sign in with your Epic Games account in order to unlock achievements. The game requests only the Basic Profile permission, which gives it access to your Epic account ID, display name and language preference. This information is processed by Epic Games to provide the sign-in and achievements features. Your achievement progress is stored by Epic Games. Epic's own privacy policy applies to that data: epicgames.com/site/privacy.

Saved game data

Your save data and settings are stored locally on your computer. They are not sent to us.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
