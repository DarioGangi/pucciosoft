Privacy Policy – Faroda

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the video game Faroda (the "Game"), published by PuccioSoft, an independent developer based in Italy ("PuccioSoft", "we", "us"). PuccioSoft is the data controller for the processing described below. Contact: pucciosoft@hotmail.com.

The Game is single-player and does not require you to create an account with us. It does not contain advertising or tracking of any kind.

1. What data the Game uses and how it is captured

a) Sign-in for achievements

When you start the Game on a platform that provides a sign-in, the Game asks you to sign in with your Epic Games account so that your achievements can be unlocked. You are shown a consent screen, and the Game requests only the Basic Profile permission. With that permission, the Game receives your account ID, your display name and your language preference. The Game does not receive your email address, password, friends list, presence information or payment data.

b) Gameplay statistics

While you play, the Game records anonymous gameplay statistics and sends them over the internet to us. For each level attempt, the data consists of:

  • the date and time of the attempt;
  • the level that was played;
  • the number of collectibles gathered;
  • the completion percentage;
  • the time spent on the level;
  • deaths in the level.

These statistics do not contain your name, account ID, email address or any other identifier of you, and we cannot link them to you.

c) Local files

The Game stores your save game and your settings (for example, audio, video and control options) on your computer. They are not sent to us.

2. How we use the data

  • Account ID: used only while the Game is running, to identify you when unlocking achievements. Display name and language are not used for any other purpose.
  • Gameplay statistics: used only to understand how players play, so that we can balance the difficulty of the levels and fix problems in the Game.
  • Save game and settings: used only to restore your progress and preferences on your computer.

We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling, marketing or automated decision-making.

3. How the data is shared

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for their own purposes. The gameplay statistics are stored in a spreadsheet hosted on Google Sheets (Google LLC), which acts as our service provider. As with any internet service, the provider may process technical connection data, such as IP addresses, in accordance with its own privacy policy; we do not have access to that data. Your account ID, display name, language, save game and settings are not sent to us or to any server operated by us.

4. How long the data is kept

  • Account ID, display name and language: we do not store them. The Game holds them in memory only while it is running, and they are discarded when you close it.
  • Gameplay statistics: kept only as long as we need them to balance and improve the Game, and in any case no longer than 24 months, after which they are deleted.
  • Save game and settings: kept on your computer until you delete them. Uninstalling the Game and deleting its save folder removes them.

5. Legal basis (EU/EEA users)

The sign-in and achievements are based on the permission you approve on the consent screen (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and on providing the feature you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by not signing in or by revoking the Game's access from your account settings on the platform you use. The gameplay statistics are anonymous; to the extent they were considered personal data, we process them on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the Game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and you may object to this processing at any time.

6. Your rights

Because the data we hold cannot be linked to you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You may still contact us at pucciosoft@hotmail.com to exercise your rights under the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) or to ask any question about this policy. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
