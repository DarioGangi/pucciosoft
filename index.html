About the game

Faroda is a hard 2D platformer with colourful pixel art and a chiptune soundtrack. Guide Faroda across the five islands of a remote archipelago, collecting scattered riches on the way to a legendary artifact.

  • Over 100 levels across five islands
  • A high but fair difficulty, with near-instant load times
  • A tutorial that teaches the core mechanics
  • Many collectible gems and hidden passages
  • Multiple endings, including a secret one for completionists
  • Full keyboard and gamepad support (gamepad recommended)
